Combining IR multiple-photon dissociation spectroscopy with DFT and minima-hopping structural sampling, we explained the size-dependent reactivity of cationic copper clusters towards hydrogen: dissociative adsorption dominates for Cu₅⁺, while Cu₄⁺, Cu₆⁺, and Cu₇⁺ show predominantly molecular binding — rationalized by transition-state barriers.
