A Python-based, automated workflow (PyFLAME) for building neural network interatomic potentials: starting only from the chemical species, it cycles between training the potential and crystal-structure prediction to grow a representative dataset with minimal human intervention, then validates the fitted potentials against reference DFT in molecular statics and dynamics.
