A machine-learned neural network interatomic potential (combined with the Morris–Ho algorithm) enabled large-scale structure prediction of near-stoichiometric MgO, mapping crystalline phases and oxygen-vacancy ordering — systems whose large unit cells are otherwise intractable at the ab initio level.
