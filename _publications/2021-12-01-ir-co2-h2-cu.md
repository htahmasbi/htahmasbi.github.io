Using IRMPD spectroscopy at the FELICE free-electron laser together with DFT reaction-path calculations, we investigated elementary steps of the copper-catalyzed CO₂ reduction: CO₂ physisorbs in an end-on configuration on cationic Cuₙ⁺ clusters and, despite dissociative H₂ adsorption, is not activated — consistent with a large dissociation barrier computed from first principles.
