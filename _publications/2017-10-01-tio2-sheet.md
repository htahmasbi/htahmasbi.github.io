Using the minima hopping method with an artificial neural network potential, we predicted a hexagonal TiO₂ nanosheet that is energetically below the experimentally known lepidocrocite monolayer and only 13 meV/atom above rutile bulk, with a large gap that can be tuned by strain and defects.
