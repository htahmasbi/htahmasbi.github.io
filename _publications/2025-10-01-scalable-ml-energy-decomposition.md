A neural network model for energy decomposition analysis: using SOAP descriptors and a custom PyTorch multilayer perceptron, it predicts electron delocalization energies of water molecules, exploiting the locality of electronic structure to scale to systems far beyond DFT reach — demonstrated on water confined in metal-organic frameworks.
