I introduced a benchmarking framework that evaluates universal MLIPs (MACE-based models, MatterSim, and PET-based models) along two axes — equation-of-state accuracy near equilibrium and global exploration via minima hopping searches. The results reveal strong performance for transition metals but systematic gaps for alkali/alkaline-earth metals, and show that smoother learned potential surfaces do not guarantee more accurate energetic landscapes: search efficiency and structural fidelity decouple.
