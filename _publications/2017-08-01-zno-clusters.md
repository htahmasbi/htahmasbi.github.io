Structural searches with the minima hopping method and a charge-equilibrated neural network potential (CENT) uncovered a novel putative tubelike ground state for the (ZnO)₃₂ cluster and 57 new metastable low-density crystalline ZnO polymorphs, whose cage-like voids resemble the hollow motifs of small clusters.
