MALA (Materials Learning Algorithms) is a data-driven framework for predicting materials properties from machine-learned models of the electronic structure, developed at CASUS and HZDR. By learning local descriptors of the electronic density, it targets accurate predictions of materials properties that capture physics beyond ground-state total energies.

**Relevance to my work.** My projects at CASUS sit next to MALA in the machine-learning-for-materials stack: benchmarking universal interatomic potentials, training domain-specific MLIPs, and building scalable ML workflows that are validated against reference ab initio and experimental data. The same discipline — checks against reliable references before trusting a prediction — governs both.
